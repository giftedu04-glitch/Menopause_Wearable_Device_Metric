# Clinical Disclaimer

**This repository is a research and engineering reference. It is not a medical
device.**

- It has **not** been cleared or approved by the FDA or any other regulator as a
  medical device.
- It **must not** be used to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or
  condition.
- All outputs are **probabilistic estimates** derived from sensors and models.
  They are explicitly **not a diagnosis** and **not a certainty**. Confidence is
  capped below 100% by design and uncertainty is always presented.
- The ingested "FDA menopausal diagnosis" reference
  (`data/fda_menopause_reference.json`) is used **only as a prior** to produce a
  *predictable* (forecast-like, uncertainty-quantified) output. It does not
  constitute a diagnosis, and it does not replace clinical judgment or
  laboratory testing.
- The illustrative hormone ranges are summaries for engineering purposes and may
  not reflect current assay- or lab-specific cut-offs. Verify against primary
  sources before any clinical use.

If you have health concerns about perimenopause or menopause, consult a
qualified healthcare professional. If you are experiencing a medical emergency,
contact emergency services.
